Ajou University CAD Laboratory

Historical laboratory introduction and research goals

The CAD Lab is an interdepartmental laboratory in the School of Engineering with four major goals:

Four major goals

  1. The development of various engineering-oriented applications using commercial/open CAD APIs for the manufacturing enterprise.
  2. The integration and synthesis of CAD/CAM/CAE/PDM/ERP systems.
  3. The study of the fundamental principles of product lifecycle management (PLM), including geometry modeling, virtual reality applications in design, and knowledge-based information systems.
  4. The education of creative programming engineers.

Laboratory history

Since its establishment in 2006, 2 Ph.D. and 9 Master students have graduated from the lab, who conduct research and educate in the areas of analysis and development of the design and information system. The laboratory seeks to establish a rational foundation for field-oriented design/information system based on a systematic understanding of the complex interactions among the many areas of information technology.

Opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students are available for thesis research and ongoing projects. There are also a limited number of postdoctoral research positions.
